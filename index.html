📖 What does "4 × 15 reps" mean?

4
Sets — do the exercise, rest, repeat 4 times total.
15
Reps — how many times you do the movement in one set.

Example: 4 × 15 push-ups = Do 15 push-ups → Rest 60 sec → Do 15 again → Repeat 4 times total.

⚠️ WEEKS 1–2: START AT HALF VOLUME

You rested for 1 year. Do NOT start at full volume. First 2 weeks: do 2 sets instead of 4, and 8–10 reps instead of 15–20. Rest 90 sec between sets. From Week 3, go full. This prevents injury and prevents quitting from extreme soreness.

Monday — PUSH
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps · 45 min

Builds the outer chest line. Hands wider than shoulders. Chest must touch ground every rep. Slow 3-second descent, explode up.

Outer Chest
How To Do It
1
Place hands 6–8 inches wider than your shoulders on the floor
2
Body straight like a plank — don't let hips sag or rise
3
Lower chest slowly (3 seconds) until it touches floor
4
Explode upward back to start. That is 1 rep.
5
Rest 60–90 seconds then do next set

Creates the chest split line down the middle. Also builds triceps to remove arm flab.

Inner Chest + Triceps
How To Do It
1
Touch index fingers and thumbs together to form a diamond shape
2
Place diamond directly under your chest, not under your face
3
Lower chest to hands. Elbows point backward not sideways
4
Press back up. Too hard? Drop knees to floor — that is fine for now

The shoulder cap builder. This is what gives Toji those round intimidating shoulders. The key exercise of the push day.

Shoulders
How To Do It
1
Start in push-up position. Walk feet toward hands until hips are high in air — like an upside down V
2
Head hanging between arms looking at feet
3
Bend elbows and lower top of head toward floor
4
Press back up to the V position. That is 1 rep.

Feet elevated on bed or chair. Targets upper chest. Gives the thick upper shelf that shows through a shirt.

Upper Chest
How To Do It
1
Put both feet on chair or bed edge behind you
2
Hands on floor shoulder width. Body angles downward
3
Lower chest to floor and press back up — same as normal push-up

Removes flab from back of arms, defines triceps. Use any sturdy chair, table edge, or bed edge.

Triceps
How To Do It
1
Sit on chair edge. Place hands on edge, fingers pointing forward
2
Slide body off chair, supporting yourself with arms straight
3
Lower until elbows hit 90 degrees. Don't go lower.
4
Press back up. Elbows point backward not sideways.
Tuesday — LEGS + GLUTES
Fix sitting damage · Reduce butt fat · 45 min

Your #1 exercise for the butt problem. After 1 year sitting, your glute muscles are switched off. This reactivates them. In 2–3 weeks you will feel them turn on. Also reshapes and firms the butt rather than keeping it soft and big.

Glutes — Fix Sitting Damage
How To Do It
1
Sit on floor. Place upper back on edge of bed or sofa
2
Feet flat on floor, knees bent at 90 degrees
3
Drive hips straight up by squeezing butt as hard as possible
4
Hold 1 second at top squeezing glutes maximum. Then lower slowly.
5
Should feel it in glutes only — not lower back. If lower back hurts, push through your heels more.

Burns fat directly from glute area while building lean muscle shape. Will feel very hard at first — that is normal. This is your butt fat reducer.

Glutes + Quads
How To Do It
1
Stand in front of chair. Place one foot behind you on the chair
2
Front foot far enough that when you lower, front knee stays above ankle
3
Lower body straight down until back knee nearly touches floor
4
Push through front heel to stand up. Do all reps one leg then switch.

Burns maximum fat from legs and glutes. The cardio element is what reduces lower body fat quickly.

Legs + Fat Burn
How To Do It
1
Feet shoulder width. Squat down until thighs parallel to floor
2
Explode upward as high as you can — leave the ground
3
Land softly by bending knees — protect your joints
4
Go straight into next squat without stopping

Builds lean quad endurance for the athletic leg look. Looks simple. Burns intensely.

Quads
How To Do It
1
Back flat against wall
2
Slide down until thighs are parallel to floor — like sitting on invisible chair
3
Hold 45 seconds. Do not let thighs rise when it burns. That burn is the point.
Wednesday — PULL + BACK
V-taper builder · Biceps · Forearm veins · 45 min

Best home back exercise. Lie under table and pull chest up to it. This builds the wide back responsible for the V-taper look.

Back + Lats
How To Do It
1
Lie under a sturdy table. Grab edge with both hands shoulder width apart
2
Body straight like a plank, heels on floor
3
Pull your chest up to the table, squeezing shoulder blades together at top
4
Lower slowly back down (3 seconds)

Fixes the bad posture from 1 year sitting. Builds lower back. After 4 weeks your posture will be noticeably better — which itself makes your whole body look more defined.

Lower Back + Posture
How To Do It
1
Lie face down on floor. Arms stretched forward above your head
2
Simultaneously lift chest, arms, AND legs off the floor
3
Hold 2 seconds at top squeezing lower back and glutes
4
Lower everything back down. That is 1 rep.

Builds the bicep peak. Veins in your hands appear as biceps grow and body fat drops — both happen from this exercise.

Biceps + Forearms
How To Do It
1
Sit in chair. Loop towel under one foot, hold both ends with same-side hand
2
Curl hand upward toward shoulder while foot pushes DOWN as resistance
3
Lower very slowly (4 seconds) — this slow part builds the most muscle
4
All reps one arm, then switch

The #1 exercise for visible forearm veins. Do this every day including rest days. Use anything heavy — water bottle, rice bag, anything.

Forearms + Vascularity
How To Do It
1
Sit. Place forearms on thighs, wrists hanging off knees
2
Hold water bottle. Curl wrists upward as far as possible
3
Lower past neutral letting wrist extend downward too
4
Do 25 reps. High rep = forearm pump = veins. Do this every single day.
Friday — CORE + ABS
Belly fat destroyer · 40 min

Foundation of all core work. If you cannot do 45 seconds, start at 20 and build up each week.

Full Core
How To Do It
1
Forearms on floor, elbows under shoulders
2
Lift body so only forearms and toes touch. Body completely straight.
3
Don't let hips sag or rise. Squeeze abs and glutes the whole time.
4
Hold 45–60 sec. Breathe normally.

Hits the obliques — the side cuts that make abs look sharp. Go slow, feel the rotation in your waist.

Obliques + Abs
How To Do It
1
Lie on back, hands lightly behind head, feet raised with knees bent
2
Bring right elbow to left knee while extending right leg straight
3
Switch — left elbow to right knee. That is 1 rep each side.
4
Go slow — feel the rotation in your waist, not just elbows moving

Destroys lower belly fat — the most stubborn area. The slow lowering is where 80% of the work happens. Never let feet touch between reps.

Lower Abs
How To Do It
1
Lie flat on back. Hands under your hips (protects lower back)
2
Raise straight legs to 90 degrees (pointing at ceiling)
3
Lower legs slowly (4 seconds) — stop just before touching floor
4
Never let feet touch until set is done. Then rest.

Cardio AND abs at the same time. The fastest belly fat burner in this program. Go as fast as humanly possible for 30 seconds.

Core + Fat Burn
How To Do It
1
Get into push-up plank position
2
Drive right knee to chest, quickly switch to left knee
3
Go as fast as possible for 30 seconds — like running horizontally
4
Rest 20 sec, repeat 4 times
Saturday — FULL BODY
Warrior conditioning · 50 min

King of full-body fat burning. Burns fat from everywhere simultaneously. If too hard, skip the push-up in the middle.

Full Body + Max Fat Burn
How To Do It
1
Stand. Squat down and place hands on floor
2
Jump both feet back to plank position
3
Do one push-up
4
Jump feet forward, then explode upward with arms overhead

Burns fat from thighs and glutes while building explosive power. Best lower body fat burner after jump squats.

Legs + Glutes
How To Do It
1
Start in lunge position — one foot forward, one back
2
Jump upward and switch legs in midair
3
Land softly in opposite lunge bending knees to absorb
4
Continue switching without stopping

The gymnast core exercise. Builds the deep core muscles that pull the stomach flat even when relaxed. This is the secret to having abs that are visible even resting.

Deep Core
How To Do It
1
Lie on back. Press lower back INTO the floor — this is the key
2
Lift shoulders and legs slightly off ground. Arms stretched overhead.
3
Hold 30 seconds. Lower back must stay pressed to floor the whole time.

Full TutorialJawline Protocol
Toji's sharp jaw comes from 3 things: low body fat that reveals the bone, developed masseter muscle that gives the square shape, and correct tongue posture that positions jaw forward. You need all 3. Every step is explained simply below — tap each one.
1
Mewing — Tongue Posture
All day every day — most important
1
Close your mouth. Lips together, teeth lightly touching or just apart.
2
Place your ENTIRE tongue flat against the roof of your mouth — not just the tip. The WHOLE tongue including the back part must press upward.
3
Your tongue presses upward with light constant pressure — not hard force, just resting flat up there.
4
This must become your default resting position always — sitting, walking, watching TV, sleeping.
5
You will forget and fall back to old position constantly at first. Just keep correcting yourself throughout the day.
Takes 2–4 weeks to become a habit. After 2–3 months the jaw visually starts moving forward and upward. This is the foundation of all jawline improvement.
Only touching roof with the TIP of tongue. The back third is the most important. Make sure the full tongue lies flat up there.
2
Nasal Breathing — Always
Never breathe through mouth — including sleep
1
During the day: whenever you catch yourself breathing through mouth, close it immediately.
2
During workouts: try to breathe through nose. It will be hard at first — your nose will adapt and open up within weeks.
3
During sleep: if you wake up with dry mouth you are mouth breathing at night. Sleep on your side — this naturally closes the mouth.
4
If nose is blocked: breathe through nose anyway. The blockage clears faster when you force nasal breathing.
Mouth breathing drops tongue from roof, widens lower face, and causes jaw to hang open — literally destroying jaw definition over years. This one habit alone creates a visible difference within weeks.
3
Hard Gum Chewing
20–30 minutes daily — masseter builder
1
Get hard chewing gum — Falim gum (Turkish brand) or mastic gum is best. Regular Wrigley or Orbit is too soft and does almost nothing.
2
Chew for 20–30 minutes continuously — your jaw muscles will fatigue and burn. That burn means muscle is growing.
3
Switch sides every 5 minutes so both sides develop equally. Do not just chew on one side.
4
You will feel jaw soreness next day — completely normal. Same as muscle soreness from working out. It means it is working.
5
Do this while watching TV or on phone — requires no extra time.
The masseter is the jaw muscle that pops out on the sides when you clench. When developed it creates the square jaw shape visible even from a distance. This is what Toji has.
Using regular soft gum like Orbit — way too soft, provides almost zero resistance. You need hard gum or mastic gum. Find it online or in Turkish/Middle Eastern shops.
4
Chin Tuck Exercise
3 × 15 reps daily — 2 minutes only
1
Stand or sit with back straight
2
Pull your chin straight backward — like you are making a double chin. Looks funny. Completely correct.
3
Hold for 5 seconds, feeling a stretch at back of neck
4
Release and repeat. Do 15 reps, 3 sets, every day. Takes 2 minutes.
5
After 4 weeks, make this your normal everyday posture — head back, chin slightly tucked while walking and sitting.
Most people do not have a bad jaw — they just have their head pushed forward from sitting, which makes neck skin fold over the jaw and hide it. Fix posture and jaw appears sharper immediately without any fat loss needed.
5
Reduce Face Puffiness
Daily habits — reveals hidden jaw within days

Face puffiness hides your jawline even when the jaw bone itself is sharp. Here is what causes it and how to fix it fast:

1
Drink 3 liters of water daily. Dehydration causes face to retain water and look puffy. More water flushes this out within days.
2
Cut salt. High salt = face retains water. Avoid chips, packaged food, fast food, instant noodles, extra salt on food.
3
No sugary drinks — soft drinks, juice, energy drinks. Switch to water only. Sugar causes inflammation and face puffiness.
4
Sleep on your back or side — sleeping face-down compresses face and causes morning puffiness every day.
5
Lose overall body fat — the fat hiding your jaw sits in cheeks and under chin. As you lose body fat from the workouts, your face sharpens automatically.
Fastest 1-week jawline improvement: drink 3L water every day, cut salt and sugar, do chin tucks. People report looking noticeably sharper within 7 days from just these habits alone.
